## A stronger director board

Before rendering the entire 24s film, create 5–8 main beat notes. Each must identify the factual line, one large hero, the human reaction, visual before/action/after, type role, motion timing, incoming/outgoing transition anchor, and real editor technique. Build the first four seconds and one difficult transformation before committing to the whole film. If opening motion reads as a stationary mockup, do not 'fix' it with a wiggling cursor. Change the subject action.

### Timestamped repairs to try, not reusable film templates

* **0–3s:** Code appears through a meaningful writing/reveal; show ownership and an imminent source-copy transition. Don't leave the same card untouched.
* **4–8s:** Make original vs unauthorized copy spatially distinct; strip/change the key with a legible action. Shorten the aftermath, and carry the copy/mark into the next shot.
* **8–11s:** Replace static centered branding by transforming a leftover shape from the previous scene into a new stage. Logo holds should be short and earned.
* **11–14s:** Obfuscation and VM should be one continuous, legible spatial transformation with staggered velocity and a clear outcome, not two diagrams with different captions.
* **19–24s:** Build the final promise word by word, hold only long enough to read and hear the ending, and finish with an audio-aligned resolution. A 4.9s near-static card is not justified by technical export success alone.

Keep intentionally quiet passages only when meaningful. Do not force fast cuts or endless animations.
