## Visible distinctions

- In the new preview around 0.8–7.0 seconds, dark code-window/card imagery repeats as the copy/post story moves forward. There is an actual duplicate, but overall structure relies heavily on composed panels, title replacements and similar dark negative space.
- At approximately 9.0–15.2 seconds, the preview changes through **SCRIPT PROTECTION**, **OBFUSCATION** and **CUSTOM VIRTUAL MACHINE**. The large text is clear but several full frames resemble the same source/VM document layout under different captions.

At 13–15 seconds a different circular grouping organizes account/device concepts. Around 17–19 seconds a paper-to-platform/upload metaphor creates another spatial mechanism; around 21–23.5 seconds a purple end composition continues a line motif.

## Decision—not slavish imitation

The improvement is not "draw more 3D" or "add more icons." It is **stronger shot architecture and more differentiated ways to change a frame**, with continuous or intentionally contrasting story beats. v67 already had the strongest tested foundation for this. Keep its scene/SFX/editing direction, and repair only repeated sharp UI contours, ambiguous UI action labels and absent owner-approved brand identity in an outro.

Use **small contextual iconography** (button/control glyphs) and deliberate subtle radii as finishing techniques. A logo in the outro is a short brand-signature element, never the story. Do not bring back the asset scouting quotas, hero SVG directives, multi-icon narrative quotas or exact storyboard from later v68–v71 versions.

## Real acceptance exercise

During a representative ad, inspect three consecutive shots *without small icons and labels*: does movement express a different cause/effect rather than only panel replacement? Inspect one transition on both sides at half speed. Verify that any icon-only button has enough context to convey its function. Verify that any site favicon is isolated, authentic and authorized. Compare to the real preview, not to a still style frame. Do not claim a motion score means creativity or prove sound mix from screenshots.
