## Runtime contract
- One real product, one premise, one surprising turn, one proof section, one satisfying callback. A second detail is allowed only in support of the same story. Preserve future episodes.
- Start immediately with a visual action or statement that creates an unresolved question. Never start with a static brand title. Large type is part of the action, not a decorative caption. Show real product proof as soon as the payoff requires it. End with a legible brand lockup held through the last spoken words.
- Map sentence clauses to actual audio timestamps. A sequence can contain multiple meaningful visual events; each event must have a readable landing. Do not confuse permanent movement with quality. Choose tightly framed close-ups, one oversize typographic shot, an intentional quiet reset, and a wide evidence shot. Avoid template monotony; inspect actual phone-size renders.
- Plan the visual motif in concrete terms: anchor, shape, color, entry position, destination, motion profile, reason. Carry one motif through at least two narrative beats. If a shared motif is irrelevant, hard cut on the narrative turn instead. Avoid generic chrome rings or random neon particles.
- Contrast: predominantly quiet neutral backgrounds, one brand accent, occasional decisive inversion. Product assets retain true colors. CGI should be a semantic accent, not the entire video. Use one or two premium treatments unless the story warrants more.
- A long sample must be a completed short-form narrative (default starting range 20–40 seconds), not a 6-second function test. Use existing voice workflow, actual voice, chosen music, script, proof, sound design, final hold, and editable source. A scratch study may benchmark an engine, but must be labeled honestly.

## Polish loop
Review the actual render at 0%, 25%, 50%, 75%, 100% of each beat. Then review temporal playback. Each section must pass: headline bounds, product readability, no unintended overlaps, coherent movement, purposeful cuts, one focus point, no accidental black frame, no unjustified pause, no fake data, actual audio locked. Check the 3 weakest moments and rerender locally. Fix priority: wrong story or claim > illegible/cropped evidence > bad rhythm > repetitive layout > effects/materials. A failed technical or semantic check blocks release; no fake numeric similarity rating. Record what changed, evidence, and remaining limitations. A new effect or working CLI is not proof of NOIZ-level craft.
