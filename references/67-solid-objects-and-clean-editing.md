## Executable art-direction contract
1. Choose a product-specific motif and write the reason it exists. It must communicate an actual benefit or tension. Do not start with a preset or repeat FORM's composition unless it is justified by the new concept.
2. Set hierarchy before effects: one primary focal point, one supporting detail, negative space around both. Use big/small contrasts, not indiscriminately large text. Keep essential UI readable. Avoid decor that competes with the sentence.
3. Build a global visual clock separate from narration. An object can persist across several speech clauses; only cut when a new semantic action or payoff warrants it. Use two or three moving planes with coherent direction and different speed, not independent random easing. Use short 4–12 frame accents and slower holds as a starting point, not a fixed formula. Keep clarity during holds; no need for all pixels to move.
4. Choose handoff condition before animating: shared position, silhouette, scale, motion vector, light or semantic match. Incoming geometry starts before it becomes visible; outgoing geometry continues until covered. Inspect four frames before and after each boundary and actual playback. Preserve motion velocity when possible. Deliberate hard cuts are allowed; don't blanket crossfade.
5. One strong CGI moment is often enough; a second needs a narrative use. If glass is chosen it must work as a solid object or meaningful magnifier. Volume examples: a rotating lens reveals proof, a crystal represents a single collected benefit, a chrome counterpart becomes transparent to show hidden information. Keep opacity/transmission high enough for plausible transparent material and avoid gratuitous scan quads. Select conservative dispersion and coherent light. Use normal opaque shapes when they serve the story better.
6. Use actual product states at payoff. Hold the key area long enough to read. Maintain consistent readable typeface, alignment, margin system, accent and lighting. Retain user-provided assets and verify safe zone. No layout label solely to fill negative space.
7. Render 3 styleframes and inspect the entire preview; select based on whether the idea is understood without narration, whether motion carries cause to effect, product legibility, and visual discipline. Review with audio; align a few real accents, do not force all cuts on beats. Record distinct issues and render fixes. Do not present objective pixel-change scores as evidence of premium aesthetics.
