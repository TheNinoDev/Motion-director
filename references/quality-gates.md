# Quality gates and revision rubric

Hard gates: final user- or agent-written script + soundtrack present/explicitly waived; actual narration present unless explicitly waived; assets legitimate; claim accuracy; output verified/clearly labeled incomplete; no copied downloader watermark/endcard; no private information; no illegible critical text; no hazardous flashing; no clipping/truncated VO. Do not disguise missing capability as artistic choice.

## Before full render
- Every scene has one focal point, audio alignment, exit/entry continuity and asset provenance. No placeholders, random stats, repeated generic template, unnecessary 3D or fake UI. Check typography bounds at full size and phone scale; intentional overscan is only for decorative content. Inspect actual 3D edges, reflections and shading before using.
- Scrub difficult shots; inspect entry/peak/exit frames; test transition boundaries and late/early caption cues. Verify project timeline length against audio and end hold. Validate plan using optional helper; it checks structure, not visual quality.

## After render
- Decode every frame/audio stream; inspect all scene boundaries and representative frames; review full motion/audio if player available. Check resolution, fps, non-corrupt streams, expected duration, correct audio mapping, peak/loudness; inspect first and last frames; no black glitches, unexpected jump cuts, truncated syllables or prolonged blank ending. Compare original voice script and final voice if audibly reviewable. Report visually/audibly unverified items honestly.
- Score each qualitative area 0–5: (1) concept/story and semantic visuals (2) typography/composition (3) timing/easing (4) transition continuity (5) meaningful variety (6) UI/3D craftsmanship (7) sound and synchronization (8) brand and CTA. A score of 4 is a target, not proof. Fix concrete sub-4 flaws when tooling allows; mark unverified rather than scoring it. Do not invent metrics or certainty.
- Revision protocol: identify issue and affected scene; preserve other approved elements; change source; rerender affected shots and final; re-run relevant QA. Distinguish design variation from repetitive technique. Never improve 'more motion' by adding meaningless movement to every layer.

## Deliverable checklist
Final video; editable native or code-based source; timing plan; dry voice and final mix if rights allow; README reproduction commands; asset manifest and QA report. Report real file paths, duration, resolution and any assumptions, failures, missing tools or unverified audio/visual quality. If final video cannot be rendered, label source-only or preview clearly.
