## A. ElevenLabs (eligible adults only)
Official site: https://elevenlabs.io/ ; product docs: https://elevenlabs.io/docs/eleven-creative/voices/voice-design ; TTS guide: https://elevenlabs.io/docs/eleven-creative/playground/text-to-speech ; EEA terms: https://elevenlabs.io/terms-of-use-eu . Current EEA terms require age 18/legal majority, and free output is non-commercial. Do not direct ineligible users to create an account, misstate age, or share accounts; use a suitable alternative. Check plan commercial rights before final use, not after editing.

Give these steps in user's language only when selected and eligible:
1. Open official site; sign up/log in, confirm email if requested. If externally authenticated, there may be no separate email verification. Never send login credentials to agent.
2. Open Voices > My Voices > Add a new voice (may be labeled Create Voice) > Voice Design. This is text-description-to-custom-voice, not Instant/Professional Voice Cloning. Find by name rather than assuming rightmost button or exact layout.
3. Paste the voice description from the agent into the voice prompt; generate previews, listen to the same sample with each voice, save best available voice. Do not choose a voice based on a random name alone.
4. Open Text to Speech, select saved voice, choose a supported expressive model, paste the agent's ready-to-copy TTS script. Use model-specific acting tags only after checking documentation. Use natural pacing/default settings initially. Generate; listen to hook and brand pronunciation. Regenerate only faulty sections if practical.
5. Use the download icon in the player or generation history. Send original MP3 or WAV directly to agent, not a screen recording or a public share link. Also send optional voice name and model for campaign consistency, never credentials.

### Original fallback voice design prompt (not a clone)
A natural adult feminine voice with a warm, softly textured mid register, close and intimate studio presence, clear consonants and relaxed conversational delivery. Understated confidence, curious and lightly playful, with subtle dry humor rather than presenter enthusiasm. Natural small breaths and varied emphasis, gently rising curiosity and a controlled confident landing. Speak like a clever friend telling a genuinely interesting short story, not a commercial announcer. Crisp and intelligible at a moderate pace, not whispery, not overly breathy, not childlike, not exaggerated anime acting. Clean dry audio, no background music, no reverb, no distortion. Adapt accent and language to the intended audience; do not imitate a specific performer.

Preview text (test only, not final campaign copy): “I thought this would take five seconds. It didn't. So I tried something different. Watch what happens now.”

## C. Production acceptance
Lock clean narration.txt separately from provider-input.txt. Stage directions only in separate performance notes or model-supported nonspoken tags. Provide exact paste block with no instructions inside the spoken text. Use one natural read by default; split only at emotional transitions or provider limits. User should not need to assemble pieces. If split, label VO-01, VO-02 etc and give a ready list. Agent checks actual decode, length, silence, clipping, audible quality and transcript against script. Regenerate wrong words or ask only for faulty take if manual. Never assume a filename or a voice description proves actual spoken words. Align the final dry voice to real timings; use phrase timing if exact words unavailable. Keep vocal consistency and avoid likely rights problems before packaging. A scratch voice is preview-only. If provider unavailable, do not restart creative discovery; reuse script and switch route.
